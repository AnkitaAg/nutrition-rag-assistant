## Summary
A diabetes-friendly recipe combining asparagus and bok choy with protein-rich eggs for a nutritious breakfast or brunch. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

## Ingredients
- Asparagus  
- Bok choy  
- Eggs  
- Oil  
- Salt and pepper (minimal) :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

## Nutrition Highlights
- Low in carbohydrates  
- Provides protein and fiber  
- Supports stable blood glucose levels :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

## Preparation Steps
1. Preheat the skillet and cook vegetables.
2. Whisk eggs and pour over veggies.
3. Broil until set and serve. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
