## Summary
This blog provides dietary tips for enhancing flavor in meals without relying on salt, which is important for individuals managing diabetes and blood pressure. Cutting back on sodium helps reduce cardiovascular risk and supports overall metabolic health. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Key Points
- The average diet often contains too much sodium. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
- Sodium reduction supports blood pressure and heart health. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Flavor Alternatives to Salt
- **Herbs and spices:** basil, oregano, thyme, rosemary  
- **Citrus zest and juice:** lemon, lime, orange  
- **Garlic and onion:** fresh or roasted  
- **Vinegars:** balsamic, apple cider, red wine  
- **Hot peppers:** cayenne, jalapeños  
- **Smoked paprika and nutritional yeast** for added depth  
- **Infused oils and aromatics** to boost taste  
- **Roasting and caramelizing** vegetables for natural sweetness :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
